About

Jennifer Piper,
Principal

J. Piper Consulting LLC is a solo practice built on more than thirty years of practical experience in project management, program administration, and organizational consulting.

Based in upstate New York, Jennifer works with nonprofits, small businesses, and individuals who need a skilled and reliable partner — someone who shows up, does the work, and delivers.

Her range extends beyond the desk: a longtime backcountry traveler and craftsperson, Jennifer also offers specialty repair and customization services through Field Gear & Finery.
